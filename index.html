02 / Confidentiality & security

Client confidentiality depends on more than a password.

A phishing email, fake Microsoft login page, stolen credentials, compromised device, malicious link or accidental exposure can put a firm's information at risk. Protection needs to follow the way your people actually work.

Our layered approach spans email, identity, devices, web access, Microsoft 365, monitoring and backup and recovery. It helps firms support their confidentiality responsibilities, including the professional context reflected in ABA Model Rule 1.6. Technology alone does not establish compliance.

04 / AI consulting

We do not begin with an AI product. We look at the business process, information, systems, integrations, privacy, security and expected value. The right answer might be AI, an agent, automation, an integration, a process improvement, existing software or a simpler non-AI solution.
