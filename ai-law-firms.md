## Audience

Small and midsize law firms in Chicagoland and the Greater Midwest. The page addresses firms that depend on responsive client communication, confidential information, Microsoft 365, secure collaboration and dependable everyday technology. It is not limited to any one practice area.

## Common problems

- Calls, texts, web chat and messages may be scattered, leaving prospective or existing clients unsure whether someone has responded.
- Phishing, fake Microsoft login pages, stolen credentials, compromised devices, malicious links and accidental exposure threaten client information.
- Staff may struggle to find the latest files, collaborate across locations or manage permissions.
- Firms may be experimenting with AI without clear use rules, information boundaries or human oversight.
- Everyday issues with devices, email, applications or printing interrupt work.

## How One I.T. Pro helps

### Communication
Connect business calls, business texting and website chat with routing and mobile response. Consider AI-assisted reception where appropriate. The aim is better responsiveness and less client frustration. [Business communication](https://www.oneitpro.com/business-communication)

### Cybersecurity and confidentiality
Use layers across email, identity, devices, web access, Microsoft 365, monitoring, backup and recovery. This can help support a firm's professional confidentiality responsibilities, including the context of ABA Model Rule 1.6. [Cybersecurity](https://www.oneitpro.com/cybersecurity)

### Collaboration
Organize Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, files, team chats, permissions and secure remote work around how the firm operates. [Business collaboration](https://www.oneitpro.com/business-collaboration)

### AI consulting and governance
Start with the business problem, then assess process, data, systems, integrations, privacy, security and expected value. The answer may be AI, an AI agent, automation, integration, process improvement, existing software or a simpler non-AI solution. Firms should establish appropriate rules for approved AI tools, client information, access, human oversight and data governance. One I.T. Pro offers an AI Usage & Data Governance Discovery for firms already using ChatGPT, Copilot or other AI tools. [AI consulting](https://www.oneitpro.com/ai-consulting)

### Everyday IT
Proactively manage the communication, collaboration and security environment, with practical help for devices, Microsoft 365 and email, applications, printing and everyday technology issues. [Everyday IT support](https://www.oneitpro.com/business-it-support)
